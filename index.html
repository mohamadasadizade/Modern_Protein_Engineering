About this course

These notes accompany a practical course on modern computational approaches to protein modelling and design. The material is organised into three connected parts: foundational concepts, AlphaFold-based structure modelling, and generative binder design using RFdiffusion.

LEARNING OBJECTIVES

By the end of the course

  • Explain the relationship between protein sequence, structure and function.
  • Describe how deep-learning architectures are used in structural biology.
  • Understand the main ideas behind AlphaFold and diffusion-based protein design.
  • Run guided structure-prediction and binder-design workflows.
  • Interpret computational outputs critically and recognise the need for experimental validation.
Important scientific note

Computational predictions and designed structures are hypotheses, not experimental proof of binding, stability or biological activity. Always interpret model confidence and validation results in context.
